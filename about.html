About Al Rehmat

Pakistan's trusted container solutions provider – built on reliability, affordability and nationwide service.

🏢 Our Story

Built on Trust & Experience

Al Rehmat Container Terminal was founded with a single vision: to make high-quality container solutions accessible to every business and individual across Pakistan.

Based in the heart of Lahore, we began by supplying containers to construction companies and quickly grew into a full-service container provider offering rental, sales, nationwide transport and custom modification services.

Over the years, we have built a reputation for fast delivery, transparent pricing and exceptional after-sales support. From a small contractor's site office to a container cafe that became a city landmark – we take pride in every project we deliver.

Our Journey

A decade of growth, learning and delivering excellence across Pakistan.

Company Founded in Lahore

Al Rehmat Container Terminal starts operations with a small fleet, supplying containers to construction companies in Lahore.

Expanded to Nationwide Delivery

Our transport fleet grew and we began delivering containers to all major cities – Karachi, Islamabad, Peshawar and beyond.

Launched Custom Modification Workshop

Introduced container modification services, enabling clients to turn standard containers into offices, cafes and homes.
