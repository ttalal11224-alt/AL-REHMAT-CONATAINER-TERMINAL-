Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. Still have a query? WhatsApp us!

What container sizes do you offer? +

We offer 10ft, 20ft, 40ft standard and 40ft High-Cube containers. Both new and quality-certified used containers are available for rent or purchase.

Do you deliver outside Lahore? +

Yes! We deliver across all of Pakistan – Karachi, Islamabad, Peshawar, Quetta, Multan, Faisalabad and beyond. Contact us for delivery charges to your city.

How long does delivery take? +

Same-day or next-day delivery is available in Lahore. For other cities, delivery typically takes 1-3 business days depending on location.

Can you modify a container into a cafe or office? +

Absolutely! We have an experienced modification team that can convert containers into cafes, offices, shops, homes and more. Contact us to discuss your project and get a custom quote.

What are your rental terms? +

We offer flexible rental periods – daily, weekly, monthly and long-term contracts. Longer rentals come with discounted rates. Delivery and pickup are included in all rental plans.

How do I get a price quote? +

The fastest way is to WhatsApp us at +92 300 8494431 or fill in the quote form above. We respond within a few hours with a detailed, transparent price breakdown.
